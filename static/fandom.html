Edit a Fandom wiki page

Sign in with a Fandom BotPassword. Your regular Fandom password will not work here. By default, the BotPassword is sent to this app server and forwarded to Fandom to start an API session, then discarded. You can save it to your Unreader account locked with a PIN of your choice; the PIN never leaves your browser and the server stores only ciphertext. The session expires after two hours by default, or 30 days if you choose to stay signed in. A server restart ends it sooner.

Create a BotPassword in your Fandom account settings under Special:BotPasswords, and grant only the editing permissions you need. Fandom's guide

Set up Fandom wiki editing

You need a BotPassword for API access. Your normal Fandom password will not work in this editor.

  1. Sign in to your Fandom account on any Fandom wiki, then open Special:BotPasswords.
  2. Create a BotPassword with a recognizable name, such as KindleEditor. Copy the generated password; Fandom shows it only once.
  3. Leave Basic rights selected. For ordinary page editing, grant Edit existing pages. Also grant Create, edit, and move pages only if you need to create pages. High-volume (bot) access is optional; use it only if you want edits marked as bot activity. Leave protected-page, CSS/JavaScript, moderation, import, deletion, and other elevated grants unchecked unless you specifically need them.
  4. Enter the BotPassword username in the form as YourFandomName@KindleEditor, along with its generated password. Enter the wiki hostname you want to edit, such as minecraft.fandom.com, then select SIGN IN. To save it, check the save option and choose a PIN.
  5. Enter a page title and select LOAD. Edit the wiki text, add a short summary, then select SAVE PAGE. Review the title and text before saving.

Fandom still checks your account's permissions on that specific wiki. A BotPassword cannot grant access your account does not already have, and protected pages may require additional wiki permissions. The same BotPassword works across Fandom wikis, but sign in again when switching to another wiki.

When you save a BotPassword, the browser encrypts it with your PIN before sending it to the server. The PIN is never sent anywhere and is separate from your Unreader password, so changing your Unreader password does not affect it. If you forget the PIN, the saved key cannot be recovered; remove it here and save it again, or revoke it at Special:BotPasswords. A longer PIN is much harder to guess than a short one. If you choose "Remember on this device", this browser keeps a key that can unlock the saved BotPassword but can never be read out or copied, so the key fills itself in and signs you in whenever you open the editor or your session ends. Untick it on a shared device, or use REMOVE SAVED KEY to forget it.
